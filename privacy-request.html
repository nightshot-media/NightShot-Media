Use this page to ask about your personal information: to access it, correct it, delete it, opt out of marketing, or ask a privacy question.

How requests work

This site has no accounts or database. The only personal information we typically hold is what you have emailed us. The form below opens your email app with a request addressed to contact@nightshot.media; nothing is sent to us until you send that email. You can also email us directly. We may ask you to confirm your identity (usually by replying from the same email address you used) and will not ask for more than we need. Requests are read and handled by Nightshot Media’s owners; we do not publish them.

Please don’t include sensitive information (such as ID numbers) here. See our Privacy Policy.

Prefer email?

Write to contact@nightshot.media with the subject “Privacy request.”
