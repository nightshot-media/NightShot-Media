1. What this website does

This website is a portfolio of our multicam video work. As currently built, it has no user accounts, no online payments, no newsletter sign-up, no comments, no file uploads, and no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Videos are hosted by Vimeo and load only after you choose to play them.

2. Information we collect

Information you send us

If you email us, or use the Contact or Privacy Request form, we receive the information you include: typically your name, email address, and the content of your message (for example event details). The forms on this site do not transmit data to our servers. They open your own email app with a message addressed to contact@nightshot.media, and the message reaches us only if you send it.

Automatically collected information

Like most websites, the service that hosts this site (our “hosting provider”) may process technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type, pages requested and timestamps in server logs, to deliver and secure the site. When you press play on a video, your browser loads Vimeo’s player from Vimeo’s servers. Vimeo may receive your IP address, browser and device information and the page you are on, and may set cookies or similar technologies. We ask Vimeo to treat your visit as “do not track” where its player supports that, but Vimeo controls what it collects. See Vimeo’s Privacy Policy. Before you have allowed it, we show a notice and Vimeo’s player does not load.

Information stored on your device

The site stores one small item in your browser’s local storage to remember your cookie/third-party-content choice. See the Cookie Policy.

Accounts and payments
