Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what cookies and similar technologies this website uses and how you can control them.

1. What they are

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device. Similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, can also keep information on your device. They are used to make sites work, remember choices, measure use, or (for some sites) show advertising.

2. What this site uses

Nightshot Media’s own site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. It does use one item of local storage, and it can load Vimeo’s video player if you choose.

Functional, analytics and advertising/marketing

We do not use functional, analytics or advertising/marketing cookies. If we add any in the future, they will be off until you consent, and we will update this page.

Third-party

The Vimeo player loads only after you press play and either allow it for that video, or allow it through Cookie Preferences. We ask Vimeo to treat the visit as “do not track” where its player supports that, but we do not control Vimeo’s cookies.

You can accept or reject optional third-party content, and change your mind later:

You can also block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Blocking Vimeo’s cookies may stop videos from playing. Changing your preference affects future page views and does not remove cookies already set by Vimeo; you can delete those in your browser.

4. Changes

We will update this page if our use of cookies changes. See also our Privacy Policy.
