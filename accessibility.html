Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Nightshot Media wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. We have built in a skip link, keyboard-operable controls with visible focus, labeled form fields, descriptive link and button text, and text/background contrast intended to meet AA. We review the site as we change it, but we cannot promise it is free of every barrier.

Known limitations

  • Portfolio videos are hosted on Vimeo and may not have captions or audio descriptions. TODO (owner): add captions on Vimeo where possible, or state the policy here.
  • Third-party content (such as the Vimeo player) has its own accessibility features, outside our control.

Tell us about a problem

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need information in another format, email contact@nightshot.media with the page address and a description of the problem. We will try to respond promptly and to provide the content another way.
